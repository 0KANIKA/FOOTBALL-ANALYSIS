Post-Transfer Minutes Sensitivity Analysis
============================================================
Primary threshold: 450 post-transfer minutes
Primary cohort size: 7000

Threshold 1500 min: n=4118, median G+A/90=0.1755, mean=0.2591, std=0.2467

Interpretation:
Increasing the minimum post-transfer minutes threshold reduces the cohort size while selecting players with greater observed playing exposure.
The 450-minute threshold remains the primary cohort because it provides the largest eligible sample among the tested thresholds while reducing extremely low-exposure observations.
Higher thresholds are treated as sensitivity cohorts rather than replacements for the primary cohort.
